Where the f* am I?

Offline car navigation to help you stay on track when GPS lets you down.

iPhone · public beta Android · APK

Navigation without GPS needs a car adapter: a small Bluetooth device that reports the car's speed. Which one to choose

Love Waze? On Android, you can share the car's position with Waze and Google Maps. How to enable it

Add a car adapter

A small OBD-II Bluetooth adapter plugs into the port under the dashboard and reports the car's speed. Combined with the phone's sensors, this helps estimate the position without GPS. Without an adapter, navigation works as long as GPS is reliable.

Android

Any Bluetooth ELM327 adapter: Bluetooth LE, or classic Bluetooth like most inexpensive ones.

iPhone

Bluetooth LE ELM327 adapters, plus OBDLink MX+ and vLinker FS / MS. Classic-only models without Apple MFi certification aren't compatible with iPhone.

Which to buy

We recommend OBDLink MX+ and vLinker FD-IOS (both tested), as well as OBDLink CX and vLinker MC+ / FD+ / FS / MS. A cheap "v1.5" clone also passed our tests, but like most classic-Bluetooth clones, it only works with Android. Avoid adapters that only work with their maker's app.

Connect once; after that it connects by itself whenever you open the app in the car. Choosing and connecting an adapter

Love Waze? So do we!

On Android, you can share the estimated position with other navigators. Keep your familiar routes, camera alerts and traffic information in Waze or Google Maps — this app supplies the position.

wtfWaze

Turn it on

Open More → Waze and Google Maps → Share the car's position. Follow the two setup steps in Android's settings — you only need to do this once.

On the road

Open this app first. Sharing starts automatically once the engine is running and the adapter is connected. You can then switch to Waze or Google Maps.

What you need

An Android phone with Google Play services and a car adapter. This experimental feature only works during drives with the adapter connected.

Install

Free, no account.

iPhone

  1. On the iPhone, open the TestFlight invitation.
  2. Install TestFlight, Apple's app for beta versions, from the App Store.
  3. No invitation code is needed. If TestFlight asks for one, close it, come back to this page and open the invitation again.
  4. Tap Accept, then Install.

Android

  1. On the phone, download the app (APK file).
  2. Open the downloaded file.
  3. If Android asks, allow installing apps from your browser, then tap Install.
  4. If Play Protect warns about an unknown app, expand More details, then tap Install anyway.

How it works

When GPS lets you down, the app works out your position from where the car started and how it moves.

Starts from a known place

It uses the last trusted GPS position or the saved parking spot. If GPS is unavailable from the start, you can set the car's position and direction manually.

Measures the car's movement

The adapter supplies speed, and the phone's sensors measure turns. Together, they tell the app how far you've driven and in which direction, even without GPS.

Matches your path to roads

The app compares the car's movement with roads on the downloaded map to work out which road you're on. No internet is needed.

Doesn't trust GPS blindly

If GPS puts you somewhere the car couldn't have reached, the app ignores it and keeps tracking your movement. When the signal is reliable again, it uses it to refine your position.

Frequently asked questions

How accurate is it without GPS?

Surprisingly accurate: in most cases, you can drive for hours without GPS and barely notice it's missing, both in the city and on the highway. Mistakes can still happen, especially if you start a drive without GPS and set the initial position or the car's heading incorrectly. If the dot is in the wrong place, stop somewhere safe and correct the position and heading manually.

Is it really free?

Yes! Not just during the beta — the app is free and will stay free forever.

Can I leave the adapter plugged into the OBD port?

Usually, yes, and many drivers do. But some cheap adapters don't go to sleep and slowly drain the car's battery. If you won't be driving for several weeks, it's best to unplug the adapter. This is especially relevant to cheap blue ELM327 clones; check your adapter manufacturer's recommendations too.
