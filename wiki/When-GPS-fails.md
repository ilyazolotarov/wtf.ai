# When GPS fails

[[Українська|When-GPS-fails-uk]]

## What the app does on its own

1. **Jamming starts.** Satellites are gone. The dot keeps moving on the car's wheel speed and the phone's turns, and
   stays on the roads.
2. **Minutes later.** Small errors add up and the circle grows. Unsure between two parallel roads, the app shows the
   other one as a hollow ring.
3. **A turn.** The turn the car made fits only one of the roads. The ring goes and the circle shrinks.
4. **Spoofing.** A GPS position that jumps somewhere the car can't have driven is ignored; a grey dot shows where GPS
   *claims* you are.
5. **GPS comes back.** Fixes are checked against the car for a few seconds before the status turns green again.

Most of the time you do nothing: **keep driving normally.**

## Put the car on the map

If the car stands and the dot is clearly on the wrong road: press and hold the map where the car really is, tap
**I'm here**, then tap the road ahead to show which way the car faces. The dot carries on from there.

## "Is the car where the dot is?"

When you set off without GPS, the app starts from where you parked. If Wi-Fi suggests somewhere else, it asks. *Yes*
keeps the parking spot; *No* lets you show where the car is. Not sure? Leave it: the question goes away once GPS
settles it.

## Without an adapter

The app can't tell how far the car goes, only how it turns. To navigate anyway:

1. Set your position on the map while the car stands.
2. Follow the route exactly. A turn off the route loses the car: then stop and set your position again.

Connect an [[adapter|Car-adapter]] before the next drive: it is what keeps the dot moving.
