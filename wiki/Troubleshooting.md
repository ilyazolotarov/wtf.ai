# Troubleshooting

[[Українська|Troubleshooting-uk]]

**The dot is on the wrong road.** If the car stands, press and hold where it really is and tap *I'm here*
([[When GPS fails]]). While driving, keep going: the next turn usually fixes it.

**The car button stays red.**

- The adapter is in the port under the dashboard.
- The ignition is on.
- Bluetooth is on in the phone's settings.
- Tap the car button, then *Connect*.
- "The adapter doesn't answer": it may be connected to another phone. Close the app there or turn off its Bluetooth,
  or unplug the adapter for a few seconds.

**The first connection takes long.** A new car can take a couple of minutes while the app finds how to talk to it.
Later connections are quick.

**iPhone: the adapter doesn't come back.** Open iOS Settings › Bluetooth and tap the adapter.

**Search finds nothing.** Search covers the downloaded region only; without one, only cities.

**"Routes reach only places inside the downloaded region."** Download your destination's region, or all of Ukraine.

**No voice.** Check the voice button beside the route's distance, the volume in Settings, and where the voice plays (press
and hold the voice button).

**Something else?** Write to SUPPORT_EMAIL and quote the *Support code* from More › Settings.
