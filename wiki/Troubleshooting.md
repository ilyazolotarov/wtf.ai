# Troubleshooting

[[Українська|Troubleshooting-uk]]

**The dot is on the wrong road.** If the car is stopped, press and hold its actual position and tap *I'm here*
([[When GPS fails]]). While driving, keep going: the next turn usually fixes it.

**The car button stays red.**

- Check that the adapter is plugged into the port under the dashboard.
- Switch on the ignition.
- Turn on Bluetooth in the phone's settings.
- Tap the car button, then *Connect*.
- "The adapter doesn't answer": it may be connected to another phone. Close the app there or turn off its Bluetooth,
  or unplug the adapter for a few seconds.

**The first connection takes a long time.** With a new car, the app may need a few minutes to work out how to
communicate with it. Later connections are faster.

**iPhone: the adapter won't reconnect.** Open iOS Settings › Bluetooth and tap the adapter.

**Search finds nothing.** Search covers only the downloaded region. Without a map, only city search is available.

**"Routes reach only places inside the downloaded region."** Download your destination's region, or all of Ukraine.

**No spoken directions.** Make sure the speaker button beside the distance on the route card isn't muted. Check
the volume in Settings. Press and hold the speaker button to check whether audio is routed to the phone or the car.

**Something else?** Write to SUPPORT_EMAIL and quote the *Support code* from More › Settings.
