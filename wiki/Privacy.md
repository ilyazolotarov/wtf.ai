# Privacy

[[Українська|Privacy-uk]]

- No account, no sign-up.
- Your position, routes, searches and trip logs **stay on your phone by default**. You decide when to share them.
- On Android, you can share the car's position with Waze, Google Maps and other navigators. They handle it under their own privacy policies.
- Testers can enable trip log upload to the developer using a personal code.
- No ads, no tracking and no sale of your data.
- Anonymous crash reports (no location, no personal data) help fix bugs.
- Maps and app updates come from the project's own server, which learns nothing about where you are.

Read the full [privacy policy](https://wtf-ai.uk/privacy.html) for details.
