# Privacy

[[Українська|Privacy-uk]]

- No account, no sign-up.
- Your position, routes, searches and trip logs **stay on your phone**.
- No ads, no tracking, nothing sold or shared.
- Anonymous crash reports (no location, no personal data) help fix bugs.
- Maps and app updates come from the project's own server, which learns nothing about where you are.

The full policy is `docs/PRIVACY.md` in the repository, also on the project's website.
