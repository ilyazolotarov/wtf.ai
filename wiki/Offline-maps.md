# Offline maps

[[Українська|Offline-maps-uk]]

The app has no online map. Each region is one download holding:

- the map,
- the road network the app uses to find the car without GPS,
- address search.

**More › Offline maps**: download, pause, delete, and switch between regions. All of Ukraine is one download, or each
oblast on its own (tens of MB each).

- **Download before the trip, on Wi-Fi.** On the road the map needs no internet.
- **Updates**: maps are rebuilt weekly from OpenStreetMap. The region you use updates by itself on Wi-Fi, or asks if
  the update is big.
- **Driving out of the region**: the map shows a card that switches to a map you have, or offers the download.

Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors. Something missing or wrong on the map? Fix it on
[openstreetmap.org](https://www.openstreetmap.org): it reaches the app with a later weekly update.
