# Offline maps

[[Українська|Offline-maps-uk]]

The app has no online map. Each region is a single download containing:

- the map,
- the road network the app uses to find the car without GPS,
- address search.

In **More › Offline maps**, you can download maps, pause downloads, delete maps and switch regions. Download all of
Ukraine in one package or choose individual regions, each taking up tens of megabytes.

- **Download before the trip, on Wi-Fi.** On the road the map needs no internet.
- **Updates**: maps are rebuilt weekly from OpenStreetMap. Your active region updates automatically over Wi-Fi,
  or asks for permission if the update is large.
- **Outside the region**: the app offers to switch to a map you've already downloaded or download the one you need.

Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors. Something missing or wrong on the map? Fix it on
[openstreetmap.org](https://www.openstreetmap.org): it reaches the app with a later weekly update.
